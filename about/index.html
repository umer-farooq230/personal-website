About Me

Background

I started programming in 2023 with no prior background — just a decision that data science was worth taking seriously. What followed was compressing a lot of ground quickly: time series econometrics, model selection, production pipeline design, and how to think carefully about what a model is actually measuring versus what it appears to measure.

I am currently studying Data Science. I am based in Lahore, Pakistan.

How I Work

My projects tend to start with a question, not a dataset. The regime switching engine started with: why should one model govern all market conditions? The IoT intrusion detection project started with: what does a complete ML workflow actually look like end-to-end? The NL2SQL fine-tuning started with: who gets blocked when databases require a technical intermediary?

I care about the difference between a model that fits data and a system that solves a problem. That usually means thinking about what the model will be used for before writing any code, and designing the pipeline and output format around that use case — not the other way around.

I also write on Medium (145 followers, published in Towards AI) because explaining something precisely is part of understanding it well.
